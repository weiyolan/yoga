Direction in short

Photo-firstEvery page opens with one big photo. Photos dominate, text stays short.
Less text“Too much text” and “too woolly” (wollig) are the most frequent complaints.
Not cheap, not clichéNo pastel or coffee-coloured palettes, no cliché yoga photos, no amateur photos.
Not cold eitherIt must radiate emotion & sympathy, not feel like “a travel agency or health insurer”.
Subtle motionQuiet hover effects, a photo that turns out to be a slow video, a custom cursor. Nothing busy or bizarre.
Retreats centralRetreats on top of the homepage; clear structure, everything quick to find.

Site structure (Structuur Website v3)

Landing at yogazentonic.com; one page per tab. Much is taken from sparklingyogaretreats.com, which Rita likes a lot.

Home

Goal: landing page with quick, easy access to all info (classes, retreats, …). Above all, get the retreats in the picture → they must be on top.

  1. Info, background, history of Yoga, Zen & Tonic: 1 paragraph
  2. Retreats
  3. Classes (Lessen)
  4. A few testimonials
  5. Who are we?
  6. Follow us: Instagram, FB

Retreats

Goal: overview of planned and past retreats.

Like sparklingyogaretreats.com: a window with a nice photo, destination and date, clickable. Plus a search filter at the bottom.

  • Coming retreats: chronological, one clickable photo per retreat. Click → short, to-the-point info, not too detailed, but visually beautiful
  • Passed retreats: chronological, similar info

Good examples

  • vita-travel.webflow.io/retreat: “Heel fijn”: photos first, then a clear overview of the info you need (screenshots under Winners)
  • nioraretreats.nl, yoga-retreat-spanje: + scroll through the photos, concise info, practical info, price and what's included. − day programme not needed

Bad example: yogapunt.be, yoga-hike-retreat-roc-du-paradis

Lessen
Classes

Goal: overview of all classes Rita gives, where and when.

Fairly structured, maybe like the retreats. Hover over (or click) the “Ashtanga” photo → short info: What is it? Why do it? For whom? Where (names of the studios where Rita teaches it).

Wat kan je volgen? (What can you follow?)

Waar kan je les volgen? (Where?)

Calendar (optional): a visual week planning showing Rita's whole schedule at once: where she teaches what.

Private coaching

Goal: briefly explain what an individual session with Rita is: what to expect, how long, etc.

A simple tab: a few nice photos of Rita with people + a short explanation, fairly to the point, but pleasant.

Team

Goal: short overview of Rita and Philippe.

A great photo with a few sentences about each person: Rita, Philippe.

See the estudioniksen.com idea: diving mask + yoga block, hover → Philippe's / Rita's face appears.

Gallery

Goal: photos that make you want to go. A mix of photos, anything goes.

Like a hotel on booking.com: photos in a mosaic, each photo with a small caption: what you see, or place of the event + date.

Example: sansararesort.com/gallery (screenshot under Winners)

Contact

Goal: a place to get in touch quickly, via email or GSM.

  • Contact form, like sparklingyogaretreats.com
  • Our details: email and GSM
  • Button to get notified about the next retreat

Open questions

  • The structure doc says “10 tabs” but describes 8 (Home, Retreats, Lessen, Private coaching, Team, Gallery, Contact + a page per retreat). Which are missing?
  • Shop: liked on sparklingyogaretreats.com. Wanted for us?
  • B&W / heavy-grain photos (oldtomcapital): a brand style or just a nice detail?
